# Teacher Guide & Answer Key — LGS Champions Cup (90')

## MEB 8th-Grade & LGS Alignment
- **Target Learner:** 13-year-old 8th-grade student preparing for LGS English.
- **Grammar & Skills:** Present Simple vs. Present Continuous, stative verbs (`know`, `want`, `prefer`, `love`, `believe`, `understand`, `need`, `has`), spelling rules (`tries`, `stopping`, `dribbling`, `taking`), schedule/stats-card reading, WhatsApp/dialogue inference, and error spotting.
- **Pacing:** Designed for a 75–90 minute study session with Post-Match Video Analysis (Mistake Review) at the end of every quarter.

---

## Complete 34-Question Answer Key

### Quarter 1: Training Ground Routines (Present Simple)
1. **f1-q1:** **B (`does`)** — "Before every training session" = routine; singular captain takes `does`.
2. **f1-q2:** **A (`practices`)** — "Every Tuesday and Thursday" + "He" -> `practices`.
3. **f1-q3:** **C (`Do / travel`)** — Plural "your teammates" takes `Do` + base verb `travel`.
4. **f1-q4:** **B (`tryes -> tries`)** — Consonant + `y` changes to `-ies` (`tries`).
5. **f1-q5:** **C (`tells`)** — Singular "My nutritionist" takes `-s` (`tells`).
6. **f1-q6:** **C (`starts`)** — Annual schedule with singular "The youth league" -> `starts`.
7. **f1-q7:** **C (`doesn't`)** — Short negative answer to `Does` question -> `doesn't`.
8. **f1-q8:** **B (`The team doesn't train on Sundays.`)** — Sunday is listed as "Rest Day (No Training)".

### Quarter 2: Live Match Commentary (Present Continuous)
1. **f2-q1:** **C (`are winning`)** — "Look!" + "We" -> `are winning`.
2. **f2-q2:** **B (`is talking`)** — "Right now" + "He" -> `is talking`.
3. **f2-q3:** **B (`stoping -> stopping`)** — Double `p` in CVC verb `stop` -> `stopping`.
4. **f2-q4:** **C (`is singing`)** — "Listen!" + singular "The stadium crowd" -> `is singing`.
5. **f2-q5:** **B (`are / taking`)** — Present Continuous question with `you` + drop silent `-e` (`taking`).
6. **f2-q6:** **C (`is playing`)** — Temporary lineup change ("this week / today") -> `is playing`.
7. **f2-q7:** **B (`is raining`)** — Ongoing rain "right now" -> `is raining`.
8. **f2-q8:** **C (`aren't`)** — Short negative answer to `Are the substitutes...` -> `aren't`.

### Quarter 3: Derby Clash & Stative Traps
1. **f3-q1:** **A (`plays / are playing`)** — "Normally" (habit) vs. "today" (temporary tactic).
2. **f3-q2:** **B (`know`)** — `know` is a stative verb; no `-ing`.
3. **f3-q3:** **C (`want`)** — `want` is a stative verb; plural "The fans" takes `want`.
4. **f3-q4:** **C (`Anatolia United has more shots than Rival FC.`)** — 8 shots vs. 3 shots.
5. **f3-q5:** **B (`am taking / come`)** — "Right now" (`am taking`) vs. "usually" (`come`).
6. **f3-q6:** **B (`am loving -> love`)** — `love` is a stative verb in LGS grammar.
7. **f3-q7:** **B (`is / talking / stops`)** — Live action (`is talking`) vs. general rule (`never stops`).
8. **f3-q8:** **B (`prefer`)** — `prefer` is a stative verb.
9. **f3-q9:** **C (`is explaining`)** — "At the moment" + singular coach -> `is explaining`.
10. **f3-q10:** **A (`has / are training`)** — Stative possession (`has`) + temporary prep (`are training`).

### Quarter 4: Championship Final (LGS Boss)
1. **f4-q1:** **D (`Who is their opponent in tomorrow's final?`)** — Not mentioned in the diary.
2. **f4-q2:** **A (`Because "believe" is a stative verb that doesn't take -ing.`)** — Mental state verb.
3. **f4-q3:** **B (`don't understand / am putting`)** — Stative `understand` + live action `am putting`.
4. **f4-q4:** **C (`Berk is wearing new boots today, and he usually hits the target.`)** — Directly supported by table.
5. **f4-q5:** **B (`is checking / is ending`)** — Both actions unfolding live right now.
6. **f4-q6:** **C (`Sentence 3`)** — "is needing" is incorrect; `need` is a stative verb (`needs`).
7. **f4-q7:** **B (`I am not hungry because I'm watching the final`)** — Natural dialogue inference.
8. **f4-q8:** **A (`scans / is finding`)** — "Always" habit (`scans`) + "right now" live action (`is finding`).
